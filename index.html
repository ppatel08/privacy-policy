Privacy Policy

PNN Studios
Last updated: September 12, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications ("Apps") published by PNN Studios ("we", "our", "us") on the Google Play Store and other platforms. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information across all of our Apps.

Data We Collect

Our Apps may collect and store the following types of information locally on your device:

  • Account information — credentials, login details, or configuration data you enter (e.g. server URLs, usernames, MAC addresses)
  • Usage data — watch history, bookmarks, favorites, and preferences stored locally on your device
  • App settings — theme, playback, notification, and display preferences
  • Downloaded content — files saved to your device storage for offline access
  • Purchase information — transaction records for in-app purchases and subscriptions, processed by Google Play (we do not store payment details)

We do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on our own servers unless explicitly stated within a specific App.

Advertising

Some of our Apps may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect and use certain data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID)
  • General location information
  • App usage data for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads. For more information, refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Some of our Apps may offer in-app purchases or subscription plans. All payments are processed through Google Play's billing system. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details, bank account information, or other financial data.

  • Subscription management: You can view, modify, or cancel subscriptions through the Google Play Store app on your device or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
  • Auto-renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period
  • Free trials: If offered, unused portions of a free trial are forfeited when a subscription is purchased

Refund Policy

Refund requests for in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play in accordance with their refund policy. To request a refund:

  • Visit your Google Play order history
  • Select the purchase you want to refund
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to submit a refund request

For purchases made within the last 48 hours, refunds are typically processed automatically. For older purchases, Google will review your request. If you need further assistance, contact us at support@pnnstudios.com.

Analytics & Tracking

Some of our Apps may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to help us understand how users interact with the App. This data is collected anonymously and may include:

  • App usage patterns and feature usage
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Device type, operating system version, and screen size

Apps that do not use analytics will state so within the App or its store listing. No analytics data is ever sold to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our Apps may connect to third-party servers or services, including but not limited to:

  • Content servers or portals specified by the user
  • Advertising networks
  • Payment processors (Google Play Billing)
  • Analytics providers

These connections are governed by the respective third party's own privacy policy. PNN Studios does not operate, control, or have access to third-party servers unless explicitly stated.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent — when you explicitly agree to share data
  • Service providers — third-party services integrated into the App (advertising, analytics) as described above
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or legal process

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption). However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Sign out or clear data from the App's Settings
  • Clear the App's data through your device's Settings > Apps
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data
  • Delete downloaded files from your device storage
  • Cancel subscriptions through Google Play

If you need assistance deleting your data, contact us at support@pnnstudios.com.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out — opt out of personalized advertising
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format

Since our Apps primarily store data locally on your device, you already have direct access to and control over your data. For any requests, contact us at support@pnnstudios.com.

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at support@pnnstudios.com.

European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process data based on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for optional features like personalized ads
  • Legitimate interest — for App functionality and improvement
  • Contract — for processing purchases and subscriptions

You may withdraw consent at any time through the App settings or by contacting us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our Apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
