Privacy Policy

PNN Studios
Last updated: September 12, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to applications published by PNN Studios ("we", "our", "us"). Our apps are client applications — they do not host, provide, or control any content. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

Our apps do NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. All data remains on your device:

  • Account credentials (portal URL, MAC address, etc.) — entered by you, stored locally on your device
  • Watch history and bookmarks — stored locally on your device
  • App preferences (theme, playback settings) — stored locally on your device
  • Downloaded content — saved to your device storage

No Analytics or Tracking

Our apps do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. No usage data is collected or sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may connect to third-party servers that you specify (such as IPTV portals). These connections are made directly between your device and the server. PNN Studios does not operate, control, or have access to these servers. The respective service provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle your data.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties. There is no data to share — nothing leaves your device to us.

Data Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. To delete it:

  • Sign out from the app's Settings to clear saved credentials
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data
  • Delete downloaded files from your device storage

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
