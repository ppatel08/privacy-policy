Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications and other software published by PNN Studios ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our applications handle information. Unless otherwise stated within a specific application, our apps are designed to operate primarily on your device and do not collect or store personal information on PNN Studios servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features of the application, certain information may be stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Account or connection information entered by you
  • Application preferences and settings
  • Favorites, bookmarks, history, or other saved application data
  • User-created or imported content
  • Downloaded files or media
  • Temporary application data required for the app to function

This information remains on your device unless a feature specifically requires communication with a third-party service.

Information We Collect

PNN Studios does not directly collect, transmit, or store personal information on our own servers unless otherwise disclosed within the applicable application.

Our applications may communicate with external services when required to provide functionality requested by you.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Unless otherwise stated within a specific application, PNN Studios applications do not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Behavioral tracking technologies
  • Third-party analytics services
  • User profiling systems

If these technologies are introduced into an application in the future, this Privacy Policy and any applicable app-store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

Some applications may allow you to connect to or interact with third-party websites, servers, APIs, services, or content providers.

These connections may include services selected or configured by you.

When your device communicates with a third-party service, information necessary for that connection may be transmitted directly between your device and the third-party provider.

PNN Studios does not control third-party services and is not responsible for their privacy practices, security practices, availability, or content.

Your use of third-party services is subject to the privacy policies and terms of those respective providers.

Permissions

Some applications may request device permissions when necessary to provide specific functionality.

Depending on the application, permissions may include access to features such as:

  • Device storage or files
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Notifications
  • Network access
  • Location
  • Photos or media

Permissions are only used for the functionality provided by the application. You can manage application permissions through your device settings.

Not every PNN Studios application uses every permission listed above.

Data Sharing

PNN Studios does not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes unless explicitly disclosed within the applicable application.

Information transmitted directly to a third-party service at your request is governed by that third party's privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by our applications generally remains on your device until you delete it.

Depending on the application, you may be able to remove data by:

  • Signing out or disconnecting an account or service
  • Clearing application data through your device settings
  • Deleting downloaded or saved files
  • Uninstalling the application

Uninstalling an application generally removes locally stored application data, although files saved outside the application's private storage may need to be deleted separately.

Security

We take reasonable measures when designing our applications to protect locally stored information.

However, no electronic storage or communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, accounts, passwords, and any third-party services you connect to our applications.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13 unless otherwise stated.

PNN Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected contrary to this policy, we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.
